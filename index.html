What it does

Three kinds of support, one thread running through them

Every part of Averun exists to close the same gap: between what you mean and what gets understood, in both directions.

Express

Find the words, in your voice

Compose messages, requests, or explanations with support that adapts to how you naturally communicate and not a generic script. Save phrases you use often and reuse them instantly, AAC-style, across text, email, or in-person prompts.

Translate

Decode social context, on demand

Idioms, sarcasm, indirect requests, unwritten social rules, Averun breaks down what's likely meant in plain, literal terms, and helps you decide how (or whether) to respond, without judgment either way.

Anchor

Steady footing through routine and change

Prepare scripts for appointments, interviews, or unfamiliar situations ahead of time. When a routine shifts, Averun helps you break the change into concrete, predictable steps instead of one overwhelming unknown.

Our approach

Support, not correction

Averun doesn't try to make anyone communicate "more normally." It gives you information and options — you decide what to do with them.

  • Co-designed, not assumed. We build features with input from autistic people and their families at every stage, not just user testing after the fact.
  • Your voice stays yours. Suggestions are optional starting points, never scripts you're pushed to follow.
  • No masking pressure. Averun explains neurotypical social context without implying it's the "right" way to communicate.
  • Plain language, always. No jargon, no vague hints, explanations are concrete and literal by default.
Trust & privacy

Communication data is sensitive. We treat it that way.

You control what's stored

Saved phrases, scripts, and history are visible and deletable at any time. Nothing is kept "just in case."

Never sold, never used to profile

Your conversations and context aren't sold to advertisers or used to build a marketing profile of you.

Clear, opt-in consent

Every feature that stores or shares data is explained plainly before it's turned on — no dark patterns, no default opt-ins.
