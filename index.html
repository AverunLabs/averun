Our mission

Build technology around what makes us human.

Averun builds human-centered AI that adapts to diverse communication and cognitive needs, helping people navigate language, context, and everyday interactions with confidence, on their own terms.

Language is one of humanity's most powerful tools for reasoning, connecting, teaching, negotiating, discovering, and caring. We want AI systems to support those abilities rather than flatten them.

Why Averun

Three principles guide everything we build.

01

Understand

Meaning is more than words. It includes context, intent, emotion, culture, uncertainty, and the relationships between people.

02

Augment

AI should expand human capability, not diminish agency. We design for collaboration between people and intelligent systems.

03

Flourish

Technology matters when it improves how people learn, communicate, decide, create, and participate in the world around them.

About

Built from language research outward.

Averun was founded by an interdisciplinary team of computational linguists, NLP researchers, psychologists, cognitive scientists, software engineers, human-computer interaction specialists, medical professionals, educators, and responsible AI experts. Together, we are building human-centered technologies that empower people to communicate more effectively, learn continuously, make better decisions, and flourish.

The work begins with a simple premise: the future of AI should be measured not only by what machines can do, but by what people are better able to do because of them.
